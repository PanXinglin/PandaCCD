## Description

Everyday moments, with a little CCD character.

PandaCCD brings camera-inspired color, fine grain and retro date stamps to your phone. Choose a clear, warm, cool or vivid look and preview it before you shoot.

Shoot and keep going
Switch looks, adjust zoom and choose your frame. Photos save by default so you can keep shooting. Prefer to edit immediately? Change the capture behavior in Settings.

Keep a moment moving
Enable Live to retain the moments around the shutter. Motion is processed locally with your selected look. Add ambient sound when you enable Live sound and grant microphone access.

Beauty you can adjust
Control smoothing, tone evening, brightening and lower-jaw shaping separately. Group photos can apply beauty to multiple detected faces. Face size, pose and occlusion affect detection; incomplete jaw geometry is not forcibly reshaped.

Edit when you feel like it
Open a photo in the in-app album to refine its look, grain, tint, vignette, brightness, crop, rotation and watermark. Save your settings or load a compatible HALD LUT.

Made for your device
Capture, editing and face detection run on the device. No account is required. Browse your photos, edit them, select multiple items, or use the system share sheet.

Availability of lenses, zoom levels, flash and Live depends on device capabilities and permissions. Live motion needs local post-processing. To share a complete Apple Live Photo, use a supported method in the system Photos app; other destinations may retain only the still image.
